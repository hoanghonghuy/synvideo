# TASK-003 — Creative Brief intake foundation

Status: BLOCKED
Milestone: F1 Creative Workflow
Depends on: TASK-002

## Goal
Create the first human-in-the-loop content intake surface where a creator can describe what they want to make and persist that intent before any expensive AI generation occurs.

## Scope preview
- Natural-language creator brief.
- Structured fields needed to separate creator facts/constraints from AI recommendations.
- Support project-level intent for short-form or long-form output without hard-coding one platform.
- Draft save/edit/reopen behavior.
- Frontend review/edit experience and durable backend persistence.
- Domain/API design ready for later text/image/video source attachments.

## Out of scope preview
- AI Proposal generation itself.
- Image/video upload processing.
- Script generation.

Full task contract will be finalized after TASK-002 review.
